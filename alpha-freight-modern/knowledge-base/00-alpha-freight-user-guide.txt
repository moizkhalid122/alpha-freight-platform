ACCOUNTS
Guest users can chat at /ai without an account (limited questions).
Suppliers need an account to post loads, pay, and manage shipments — signup at /auth/modes then Road, role supplier.
Carriers need an account to bid, deliver, and receive payouts — signup at /auth/modes then Road, role carrier.
After signup complete /onboarding — approval usually 1-2 business days.
Air freight uses separate portal: /auth/air/signup and /auth/air/login.

SUPPLIER WORKFLOW
Post load at /supplier/post-load with route, cargo, dates, equipment, budget.
Review bids at /supplier/my-bids — accept to book carrier.
Pay Instant /supplier/pay-instant or Pay Later /supplier/pay-later via Stripe.
Track at /supplier/track · Refunds /supplier/refunds · Wallet /supplier/wallet.

CARRIER WORKFLOW
Browse /carrier/available-loads and /carrier/smart-loads · bid via /carrier/my-bids.
Execute assigned loads in /carrier/my-loads · upload POD at delivery.
Wallet /carrier/wallet · Payout setup /carrier/wallet/payout-setup · 7-day payout target after POD.
Vehicles /carrier/vehicles · Drivers /carrier/driver-panel · Earnings /carrier/earnings.
